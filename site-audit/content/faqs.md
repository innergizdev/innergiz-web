-   **Q: How can Coaching benefit our organisation?  
    **A: Executive coaching benefits organizations by enhancing leadership skills, fostering improved performance, promoting effective communication, increased employee engagement, talent retention and strategic planning.
-   **Q: How can life coaching benefit me personally and professionally?  
    **A: Life coaching empowers individuals to set and achieve goals, overcome obstacles, and create a more fulfilling and balanced life.
-   **Q: What is the typical duration of a life coaching engagement?  
    **A: The duration varies based on individual needs, but many clients experience positive changes within a few months of consistent coaching sessions.
-   **Q: How do I get started with life coaching sessions?  
    **A: Begin by scheduling an initial consultation with one of our experienced life coaches, by filling the ‘Contact Us’ form. This session helps identify your goals and determine the best coaching plan for you.
-   **Q: What are the areas on which an individual can work upon by taking services of an ICF credentialed coach?  
    **A: An ICF (International Coach Federation) credentialed coach can assist individuals in various areas, including personal development, career advancement, leadership skills, stress management, work-life balance, and goal achievement. Through a structured coaching process, individuals can gain clarity, set actionable goals, and receive guidance to navigate challenges and optimize their personal and professional lives.
-   **Q: What do you provide under your mentor coaching program?  
    **A: In our Mentor Coaching program, coaches receive guidance and feedback from experienced mentors to enhance their coaching skills, adhere to ethical standards, and work towards achieving or renewing their ICF credentials. The program includes 10.5 hours of group and individual mentor coaching. Contact us for further details about this program.
