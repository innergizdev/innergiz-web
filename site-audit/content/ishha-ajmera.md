## Ishha AjmeraExecutive Coach | Corporate Trainer

![](https://interaxis.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/8.jpg)

I am an Associate Certified Coach from ICF with almost 20 years of experience as an Entrepreneur and Coach. I specialize in enhancing the leadership skills of founders and mid-senior managers, which positively impacts organizational effectiveness. I currently serve a diverse range of clients and consult with organizations on leadership development and performance improvement, leveraging my corporate background and coaching expertise to understand and address business challenges.

My areas of strength include self-mastery, transformational leadership, team integration, communication influence, executive presence, and holistic wellness. The breadth and depth of my experience are core assets that I bring to individuals and organizations alike. Some of the organizations I have worked with include Aditya Birla Capital, Deutsche Bank, Dish TV, ICICI Securities, Impartus, Impetus, Magicbricks, SRF, TATA AIG, and UBER. I have provided consulting services to select organizations to assist them in achieving their strategic objectives. I firmly believe that every individual has the potential and capability beyond their realization and am committed to working with them to help them become the best version of themselves.
