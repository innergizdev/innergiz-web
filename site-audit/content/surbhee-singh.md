## Surbhee SinghFOUNDER | MASTER CERTIFIED COACH (MCC-ICF)

![](https://interaxis.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/6.jpg)

I am the Founder and CEO of a niche Leadership Development Company, Innergiz Consulting Group. I use my diverse corporate experience to engage with C-suite leaders and key team members to identify strategic areas that can catapult their performance. I devise coaching interventions and customized training to address such areas that help these leaders adapt and align themselves to achieve their goals effectively and improve productivity and revenue.

I enable leaders and mid to senior-level managers to enhance their self-awareness and have a greater impact in an increasingly globalized business environment. My areas of developmental focus include coaching, coach mentoring, transition coaching, inner transformation, leadership development, communication influence, executive presence, self-mastery, story-telling, team integration, influence without authority, and holistic wellness.
