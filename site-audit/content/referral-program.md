# Innergiz Coachmate Referral Program

Refer a Friend. Reward a Dream. Get XYZ.

Do you know someone who's thinking about becoming a professional or business coach? Introduce them to Innergiz Consulting Solutions and our globally accredited coach education programs—and we'll thank you with XYZ when they enroll!

## Why Refer?

It's more than a reward (though, who doesn't love a great reward?). You're giving someone the gift of transformation, helping them kickstart a meaningful career in coaching—guided by experienced faculty and backed by international accreditations.

## How It Works

1

### SHARE

Let us know who you're referring by filling out the form below. Please only refer people you've spoken to and who would be open to being contacted by Innergiz.

2

### CONNECT

We'll reach out to them, answer any questions, and guide them through the enrollment process.

3

### REWARD

Once they enroll in any of our eligible coaching programs, we'll send you XYZ as a thank you!

## Submit Your Referral

### Quick Rules

-   Only new contacts (not already in touch with Innergiz) are eligible
-   You may refer up to 5 people per month
-   Rewards apply only to ICAP Level 1, ICAP Level 2, and ICAP Integrated programs
-   Not valid for youth programs, short courses, or corporate-sponsored enrollments
-   Referrals must enroll within 90 days of submission to qualify
