## Sidhartha BhasinLeadership & Team Coach | Strategy & Operations Leader

![](https://interaxis.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/1.jpg)

I am a seasoned mentor and an experienced leadership & team coach with a solution focused approach of linking business outcomes to leadership and team effectiveness. I bring together ancient wisdom of the East with coaching techniques of the West to enable my clients to achieve their desired outcomes. I have coached more than 200+ leaders, facilitated over 350+ workshops, and worked with clients of 18+ nationalities across the globe. For my work in Coaching and Human Resources,  
I have been recognized and awarded as Best Career Coach (2021) and Emerging HR Leader (2016). I lead HR Strategy and Operations for Googler Experience at Google Asia Pacific. Prior to this role, I led a team of highly competent HR Business Partners across the region for Google’s Products, Platforms, and Services business lines.  
Previously, I have worked with Standard Chartered Bank as the HR Lead for Corporate & Institutional Banking, HR Strategy Lead with Ocwen Financials, and Senior HR Consultant with Aon.

#### Certifications

-   Professional Certified Coach (PCC) accreditation from International Coaching Federation (ICF)
-   Trained in coaching techniques and tools with Erickson Coaching International, Co-Active Training Institute (CTI), and Positive Intelligence
-   Certified Hogan Assessor
-   Leadership Circle Practitioner
-   Demartini Method Practitioner
-   Certified Clifton Strengths Facilitator
-   Certified in other 5 factor models (e.g. MBTI)
